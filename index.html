About Me

A Teacher Who Makes English Click

With over 1 years of classroom experience across primary, secondary I have dedicated my career to making the English language accessible, exciting, and transformative for every student I meet.

My teaching philosophy centres on building confidence first. I believe that when students feel safe to make mistakes, they learn faster, retain more, and develop a genuine love for the language. I blend structured grammar lessons with real-world communication activities, creative writing, and critical thinking exercises.

I hold a Higher National Diploma (HND) in English, and have completed advanced training in language assessment and curriculum design. Whether a student is preparing for national exams or simply wants to improve their everyday English, I tailor each lesson to their unique goals.
